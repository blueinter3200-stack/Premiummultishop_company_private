## Use / next verification

1. First verify current employment and exact title on the LinkedIn page itself or an authorized public extractor; search snippets can merge multiple profiles.
2. For A-ranked people, verify whether the role owns new-brand sourcing, category buying, import registration, or only sales/marketing.
3. Verify company website, current brand portfolio, importing entity, target country coverage, MOQ/payment expectations, and regulated-product registration before any approach.
4. Do not treat any profile post as evidence of willingness to buy; use it only as a factual personalization hook after re-opening the source.
